Yesterday I gave an introductory talk on Guix as a package manager in the
context of foreign distributions, specifically Debian. I illustrated the main
characteristics of the system for absolute beginners in the context of
transactional and functional packaging systems. I returned after some years to
the LinuxDay event with a technical presentation. 

![A view of one of the rooms, photo by Michele Rizzato](/images/ld2026.jpg)
_(Ph. Michele Rizzato)_

In my latest presentations at such events, I have always preferred more
political and general-purpose topics.  It's been nice to return with a
_for-nerds-only_ theme, a giant leap back to the past.

The slides of the talk (italian only) are available [here](/downloads/LD2026-guix.pdf).
